This Privacy Policy explains what information the app AR Trace Draw – Camera Sketch (the "App"), published on Google Play by Pedro Rodríguez ("we", "us"), accesses, collects, uses and shares, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • We (the developer) do not run any server and do not receive your photos, drawings, name, email or any account data.
  • Camera images and gallery images are processed only on your device.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob, a third party, collects some device data (described below) to serve ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play.

3. Data collected and shared by third parties

3.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

For users of the free version, the App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display ads. The SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic/performance data of the ads SDK.

This data is used for advertising, analytics of ad delivery, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform, to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Privacy settings button on the App's home screen.

Opting out of personalized ads: you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

Users who buy the premium version do not see ads, and the ads SDK is not used to show them ads.

3.2 Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We never receive your payment details. The App only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid.

3.3 Google Fonts

The App may download fonts from Google Fonts servers the first time they are needed. This request reveals your IP address to Google. No other personal data is sent.

3.4 Google Play in-app review

If you choose to rate the App from inside it, the review is handled by Google Play under your Google account. We do not receive your identity from that flow.

4. Data we (the developer) collect

We do not collect, store or have access to any personal data through the App. We do not sell personal data. If you email us, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you.

5. Data security

The App does not send your images or content off your device. Data collected by the third-party SDKs above is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections and handled under Google's security practices.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Data stored on your device (settings, premium flag, saved images) stays there until you delete it. You can delete it by clearing the App's data, uninstalling the App, or deleting the images from your gallery.
  • Data collected by AdMob and Google Play is kept by Google according to its own retention policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). You can manage it in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.
  • Emails you send us are deleted on request. To request this, write to the contact address above.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK, CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data from the App, most requests are best handled through the in-app Privacy settings and your Google account. You can contact us with any request. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page and the "last updated" date will change.
